# Electivity demo

A guided demo of **Electivity**, a course planner a school generates from its own schedule spreadsheets:
students search and filter electives, see time conflicts before they add a course, track credits
against their program's rules, view everything on a calendar, and copy their course numbers at registration.

**Try it:** https://amithharsha.github.io/electivity-demo/

The catalog, courses, instructors, dates and course numbers in this demo are fictional.
"Your school here" marks where a school's own name and logo go.
